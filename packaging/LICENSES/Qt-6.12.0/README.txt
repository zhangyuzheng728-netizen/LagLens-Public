Qt 6.12.0 third-party license texts
===================================

The `Qt-6.12.0` folder contains license texts retrieved from the official
Qt repositories at tag v6.12.0 on 2026-11-13:

- qtbase/LICENSES          https://github.com/qt/qtbase/tree/v6.12.0/LICENSES
- qtdeclarative/LICENSES   https://github.com/qt/qtdeclarative/tree/v6.12.0/LICENSES
- qtvirtualkeyboard/LICENSES
                           https://github.com/qt/qtvirtualkeyboard/tree/v6.12.0/LICENSES

Qt PDF is built from Qt WebEngine sources. Its third-party component
list includes PDFium, Chromium, Abseil, FreeType, ICU, libjpeg-turbo,
libpng and zlib. The authoritative list and licensing details are
maintained by The Qt Company:

  https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/licenses-used-in-qt.html

The Qt libraries are unmodified upstream binaries from the PySide6
6.12.0 wheel. Corresponding Qt/PySide6 source is available at:

  https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/
  https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/

The Qt PDF third-party notice bundle should be reviewed before a public
binary release. LagLens does not modify Qt or its third-party components.
